About Me

Hello! My name is Dallin Doxey and I’m a passionate Sound Designer who’s loved playing games my entire life. My love for audio sound design started with an interest in music as a part of the medium. I grew to understand how vital audio was to these experiences I loved. This interest in composition and sound design lead me to pursue a degree in Digital Audio at Utah Valley University. I graduated in 2024 with a hunger to learn more and to contribute to this medium I adore. I've worked with small indie teams and game jams to further my understanding of audio implementation since I graduated and I'm excited to find my next opportunity in my career!

Featured Work

Demo Reel — This is a sound redesign of three games in three different states of developement. Starting with the trailer for Amnesia: The Bunker. This showcases my attention to detail in using audio to express in small clips how the full product would make you feel. I feel this showcases how I approach sound in different locations while still keeping the games general theme. The next is a showcase of my sound design while working in UI, I had a blast working on this as it was great to have such a straightforward way to work on player feedback using audio. Very happy with how it turned out. The last is the longest and showcases my sound design in gameplay using XCOM 2. There are many subtle additions I've added here such as a quiet clicking sound with variation when hovering over cover as it's a vital part of XCOM's gameplay loop. Icons popping up making sound in order to further show player input and the general ambiance and movement of the characters. I'm happy with how my Demo Reel turned out and I'm exctied to only interate on it and grow as a Sound Designer!

Escape The Office VR — I worked mainly as the Audio Implementation portion of this project. I worked in Unity and Wwise to program this game's audio system. This was a very difficult and interesting challenge as I had to program for a VR player. I haven't done so before but even outside of VR I feel I learned a great deal about how to work with constants in programming and accounting for player action. When working in VR there are many moving pieces that can be happening all at once so taking that into account led in part to the game turning out how it did. I'm happy I was able to contribute to this project!

Coyote Kills the Giant — This was an animated short film that I took over and worked on the audio from the ground up within a tight deadline. I was given two weeks to complete this project which was difficult to juggle with a full time job but I managed it and had a great time all at once! This was a great example of how I breakdown a project in a short time frame to make sure I can achieve every goal I can. I prefer to begin on the most challenging aspects first, which in this case would be the climax of the short film then working around that. Mapping out what I can of emotional importance is key to making sure there are memorable moments for your audience and this project exemplifies that greatly. I'm very glad I was brought onto this project and I'm happy with how it sounds even with the constraints!

Love Josh — This short film I was given complete creative control over as the Lead Sound Mixer and it was so much fun. I recorded everything on the set and was there through every part of the process audio wise. I also handled the post production and score for this and it was just a blast to really get my creative juices flowing. I learned a lot about time management with these projects as there are strict deadlines that need to be met, working with other people on a long form project, and taking full responsiblity for a large portion of a production.
